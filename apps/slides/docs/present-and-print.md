# Present and print

## Open a presentation

On the home screen:

- **Choose a folder** opens the browser's folder picker. Your browser may ask you to confirm that
  it can read the folder: that only gives Slides read access to what is in it, for this visit.
- **Drop** a folder anywhere on the page.
- **Load the example** opens the bundled example, in the language of the interface.

If the folder has no `slides.md` at its root, or its settings are not valid, Slides says what is
wrong and stays on the home screen. **Close the presentation** (the cross at the right of the control
bar) goes back to it, where you can open another one.

## Move through the slides

| Key                            | Action                                           |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| `→`, `↓`, `Space`, `Page down` | Next slide                                       |
| `←`, `↑`, `Page up`            | Previous slide                                   |
| `Home`, `End`                  | First and last slide                             |
| digits, then `Enter`           | Go to that number: `1`, `2`, `Enter` is slide 12 |

The control bar has the same moves: previous and next, a **slider**, the **counter**, and a field
to type a slide number. The bar is at the bottom of the window.

- **Fit the slide to the window** (`R`) switches between the 16:9 frame and the whole window.
- **Full screen** (`F`) uses the whole display.

## Overview

**Overview** (`O`) shows every slide as a card with its number, title and first sentence; the
current one is highlighted. Choose a card to go to its slide. `Esc` or `O` closes it.

## Presenter window

**Presenter view** (`S`) opens a second window, which you put on your own screen while the audience
sees the first one:

- the **current slide** and, under **Next slide**, the title and first sentence of the following one;
- the **presenter notes** and the **public notes** of the current slide;
- a **total timer** that you start, pause and reset, and a **slide timer** that starts again at every
  slide;
- the **Previous** and **Next** buttons, and the arrow keys, which move the main window.

The two windows follow each other: moving in one moves the other. They talk through the browser,
without a server, so both must stay open in the same browser. If you open the presenter window
before a presentation is open, it waits and shows the presentation as soon as there is one. The
timers belong to the presenter window: closing it or reloading it resets them.

Pop-up blockers may stop the second window: allow pop-ups for the site if nothing opens.

## Print

**Print** (or `Ctrl+P`, `⌘P`) prints **one A4 page per slide**: the slide in the upper half, its
**public notes** in the lower half. The paper is always light, whatever the theme of the slides and
of the app, and diagrams are drawn first. Presenter notes are never printed. In the print dialog,
choose "Save as PDF" to get a file.
