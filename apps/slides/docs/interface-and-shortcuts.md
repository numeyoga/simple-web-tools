# Interface and shortcuts

## The screens

- **Home screen**: the sidebar (the Slides brand, and the links to the other apps of the suite), the
  top bar with the **Preferences**, and the ways to open a presentation. Below 1024 px the sidebar is
  a drawer opened with the **Menu** button.
- **Presentation**: the slide fills the window, with the control bar at the bottom: previous, a
  slider, the counter, a "go to" field, next, then **Overview**, **Presenter view**, **Print**,
  **Fit the slide to the window**, **Full screen**, **Preferences** and **Close the presentation**.
- **Presenter window**: the bar with the timers, the current slide, the next slide, the notes and the
  buttons to move.

## Shortcuts

| Key                         | Action                                       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `→` `↓` `Space` `Page down` | Next slide                                   |
| `←` `↑` `Page up`           | Previous slide                               |
| `Home`, `End`               | First, last slide                            |
| digits, then `Enter`        | Go to that slide number                      |
| `O`                         | Overview. `Esc` closes it.                   |
| `S`                         | Presenter window                             |
| `R`                         | Fit the slide to the window, or back to 16:9 |
| `F`                         | Full screen                                  |
| `Ctrl+P`, `⌘P`              | Print                                        |

`O`, `S`, `R`, `F` and the digits are **letter and digit shortcuts**: they can be turned off in the
preferences, and they never fire while you type in a field, when a dialog is open, or with `Ctrl`,
`Alt` or `⌘` held (WCAG 2.1.4). The arrows, `Space`, `Home`, `End` and the page keys always work. On
a button or a link, `Space` and `Enter` press it instead of moving to the next slide.

## Preferences

- **Language**: automatic (your browser's), English or French. Changing it reloads the page.
- **Theme**: system, light or dark. It styles the app, not your slides: a presentation sets its own
  theme in its settings.
- **Text size**: automatic, standard, comfortable or large.
- **Letter and digit shortcuts**: on or off.
