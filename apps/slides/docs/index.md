# Slides documentation

User documentation for Slides, in English. The interface also exists in French; labels are given in
English here.

- [Write your slides](write-your-slides.md): the folder, `slides.md`, the frontmatter, the notes,
  code, diagrams and images.
- [Present and print](present-and-print.md): open a folder, move through the slides, the overview,
  the presenter window and its timers, the print view.
- [Privacy, offline and what is blocked](privacy-and-offline.md): what the app reads, what it
  never sends, why a remote image is not shown, and how it works offline.
- [Interface and shortcuts](interface-and-shortcuts.md): the screens, the keyboard, the
  preferences.
