# Site icons and privacy

Favoris has no server and no account. Your bookmarks are stored in your browser, and nothing is
sent anywhere.

By default each link shows a **coloured initial**: the first letter of its site, on the colour of
its group.

## Load site icons from Google

In the preferences you can turn on **Load site icons from Google**. It is **off by default**. When
it is on, your browser asks Google's favicon service for one icon per link. That tells Google the
**domain** of each link (for example `example.org`, not the path) and your IP address. Google sees
the domains of your links.

Turn it off and the icons disappear at once, and nothing more is requested. If an icon cannot be
loaded, the initial stays.
