# Organize your bookmarks

## Add a link

Choose **New link** (or press `N`). Give an address, and optionally a name and a group. You can
leave out `https://`. Only web addresses are accepted: `http` and `https`. An address such as
`javascript:...`, `file:...` or `mailto:...` is refused with a message.

Choose **New group...** in the group list to make a group on the spot.

## Groups

- Open the menu **More actions** and choose **New group**, or use the group list of the link form.
- Each group has one of 16 colours. The colour is always shown next to the name of the group.
- The star puts a group first. The arrow in its title folds it; the choice is remembered.
- In **edit mode**, the menu of a group lets you edit it (name and colour), move it up or down, or
  delete it. Deleting a group asks first and keeps its links, with no group: they appear in the
  card **Ungrouped**.

## Search

Type in the search field, or press `/` or `Ctrl+K`. It looks at names, addresses and group labels,
ignoring case and accents. Only the groups with a match are shown.

## Reorder

Turn on **Edit** (the pencil button). Then either:

- **drag** the handle at the left of a link to put it before or after another link, or onto the
  title of another group; drag the handle of a group to move the group; or
- use the keyboard: each link has a **Move** menu with **Move up**, **Move down** and **Move to a
  group...**, and each group has a menu with **Move up** and **Move down**.

The order is kept.

## Workspaces

A workspace is a separate page of bookmarks, for example for work and for home. Open the workspace
button at the top of the sidebar to create, switch, rename, duplicate or delete them. The last
workspace and the active one cannot be deleted.

## Several tabs

Only one tab can change your bookmarks at a time. The others show a banner and are read-only; they
follow the changes of the first one, and one of them takes over when it is closed.
