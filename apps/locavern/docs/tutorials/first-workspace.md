# Create your first workspace

In this tutorial you create your first project in Locavern, add a task with a checklist, move it
across the Kanban board, write a linked note, sketch a whiteboard and export a ZIP backup.
Everything stays stored locally in your browser.

The interface is available in English and French. It follows your browser language and can be
changed in the **Language** field of the display preferences (**Display**, under **Preferences** in the side navigation; see [Interface, views and shortcuts](../reference/interface-and-shortcuts.md)).
This tutorial uses the English labels.

## 1. Create a first project

Open Locavern in your browser. On first use the **Tasks** view appears and asks you to create your
first project.

1. Click **Create a project**. The **New project** window opens.
2. In **Project name**, type `Office renovation`.
3. In **Description**, type `Layout and equipment follow-up`.
4. Click **Create project**.

A **Project created** notification appears at the bottom of the screen. The project selector at
the top left shows `Office renovation` and the view says **No tasks found**.

## 2. Add a detailed task

1. Click **New task**. The **New task** side drawer opens on the right.
2. In **Title**, type `Order the desk`.
3. In **Priority**, select `High`.
4. In **Due date and time**, choose the date `2026-12-01`.
5. Under **Checklist**, type `Compare three models` in **New checklist step**, then click **Add**.
   The step appears with a checkbox.
6. In **Description**, click the text area and type `Prefer a 160 cm top.`
7. Click **Create task**.

A **Task created** notification appears and the drawer closes. The task table now has a row for
`Order the desk` with the status `To do`, the priority `High` and the due date `2026-12-01`.

## 3. Move the task on the Kanban board

1. In the header of the **Tasks** view, click **Kanban** in the display mode selector.
2. Three columns appear: **To do** (counter `1`), **In progress** (`0`) and **Done** (`0`).
3. On the `Order the desk` card, click the actions button (**⋮**) at the top right of the card.
4. Click **Move to In progress**.

The card moves to the **In progress** column, whose counter becomes `1`, and **To do** goes back
to `0`.

## 4. Write a linked note

1. In the side navigation on the left, click **Notes**. The screen says **No notes yet**.
2. Click **Create a note**. The **New note** drawer opens with `Office renovation` already selected
   in **Project**.
3. In **Note title**, type `Desk dimensions`.
4. In **Content**, click the text area under the toolbar and type `Available width: 180 cm.`
5. Click **Create note**.

A **Note created** notification appears and the drawer closes. The note `Desk dimensions` is
selected in the list on the left and open in the built-in editor, with the status
**Saved locally**.

## 5. Sketch a linked whiteboard

1. In the side navigation, click **Whiteboards**. The screen says **No whiteboards yet**.
2. Click **Create a whiteboard**. The **New whiteboard** window opens with `Office renovation`
   already selected in **Project**.
3. In **Whiteboard title**, type `Floor plan`.
4. Click **Create whiteboard**. The canvas of `Floor plan` appears.
5. In the canvas toolbar, select **Sticky note**, then click **Add to center**.
6. Press `Enter` or `F2` on the selected sticky note, replace the text with
   `160 cm desk near the window`, then click **Confirm**.

The status at the bottom left says **Saved locally**.

## 6. Export your first ZIP backup

1. In the top bar, click **Back up**. The **Backups and storage** window opens.
2. Under **Local storage status**, check the counters: `1` project, `1` task, `1` note and
   `1` whiteboard.
3. Under **Export an archive**, click **Export ZIP archive**.

An **Archive exported** notification confirms it, and your browser saves a file named
`locavern-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.zip` (`locavern-sauvegarde-AAAA-MM-JJ.zip` when the interface is in
French).
